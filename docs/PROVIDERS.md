# Real API Providers

## Video

| Provider | ID in Settings | What it does |
|----------|----------------|--------------|
| **fal.ai Kling** | `fal` | Best quality text-to-video & image-to-video |
| **OpenAI Compatible** | `openai` | Any Sora-style / OpenAI-compatible endpoint |
| **Local ComfyUI** | `local-comfy` | Point to your local instance (workflow still needs to be added) |

## Audio

| Provider | ID in Settings | What it does |
|----------|----------------|--------------|
| **ElevenLabs** | `elevenlabs` | TTS, Music, Sound Effects |

## How to use

1. Open **AI Settings** panel
2. Paste your keys:
   - fal.ai → `fal.apiKey`
   - ElevenLabs → `elevenlabs.apiKey`
   - OpenAI-compatible → `openai.apiKey` + `openai.baseUrl`
3. Go to AI Video or AI Audio panel and generate

Keys are stored only on your device and tied to your local account.
