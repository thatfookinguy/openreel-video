# About MarketXyn Studio

**MarketXyn Studio** is an AI-powered creative studio for affiliate marketers, creators, and operators.

### Tagline
**Create. Convert. Scale.**

### What we believe
- Speed without sacrificing quality
- Privacy by default (your media stays local)
- Bring your own keys — no lock-in
- One system from product research to finished video

### Built on open source
The professional video editing engine is based on OpenReel Video (MIT License).  
MarketXyn Studio extends it with generative AI, multi-provider APIs, local models, authentication, and a full affiliate production pipeline.
