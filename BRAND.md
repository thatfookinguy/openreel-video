## Positioning
MarketXyn Studio is a privacy-first, AI-powered creative studio built for affiliate marketers, creators, and operators who need to turn products into high-converting short-form video — fast.

It is based on the open-source OpenReel video engine (MIT) and extended with generative AI, multi-provider APIs, local LLMs, and a full affiliate production pipeline.
